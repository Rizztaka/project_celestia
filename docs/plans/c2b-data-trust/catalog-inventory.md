# C2b catalog inventory

Audited 2026-10-02 by Codex. Maintenance owner for every catalog is the Genshin game domain; game-data review remains the project owner's responsibility. This inventories the ten catalogs present at phase start, not current-patch coverage.

Endgame services also contain fixed reward/tier/scoring assumptions outside these ten files. They remain legacy unverified inputs; C2b does not validate rotations, rewards, scoring, or endgame coverage.

New static files: `material-references.json` stores the five canonical consumed-key/name/game-item-ID joins for the sourced talent slice; `catalog-evidence.json` binds its exact range and dependencies; `advice-domains.json` has no eligible routes. Validation lives in `companion/catalog-trust.ts`. Goal creation uses target identity separately from advice eligibility, including existing unsupported goals. Imported unknown keys remain opaque and preserved.

Cadence/resin observations are owned by the existing C2a provider policy and its evidence record. Commission/Teapot reminders, manual resin and Transformer use, and aggregate weekly claims do not consult the cost catalog. C2a validation does not establish farming resin-per-claim rules, cooldowns, or rewards.
