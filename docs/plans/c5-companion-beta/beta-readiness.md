## Decision and ownership

Local code/test results justify a focused C5 review, with the accessibility limitations above remaining open. Do not invite testers or deploy from this checkpoint. The project owner must assign named owners and verify the recovery, exposure, data-control, upkeep and cutover prerequisites; documentation by itself closes none of them. A small synthetic local demo does not need a shared writable guest account, and none was added.

Once operational/accessibility prerequisites are met and invitations are authorized, collect voluntary small-group observations. Human feedback determines whether the broader C5 beta exit criterion is met. **C6 snapshots/teams and C7 event/wish-savings work remain gated on real beta feedback.** No next milestone starts automatically.
